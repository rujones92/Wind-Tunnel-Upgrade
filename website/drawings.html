GD&T drawings

35 ANSI B sheets, generated from the CAD solids: five assembly sheets with parts lists (100-500) and 30 part sheets. Series 0xx: balance, puck, sting. 2xx: roll spindle. 3xx: calibration hardware (308-310 are the pulley uprights, axles and pulleys). 4xx: motorized pitch sector. 5xx: yaw carriage (preliminary). The part views are true hidden-line views, hatched sections, ASME Y14.5-2018 feature control frames and datums. Download the full set (PDF).

REV A, AI-generated: the CHECKED and APPROVED blocks are blank on purpose. Have a qualified engineer or the machine shop review fits, tolerances and GD&T, and confirm the vendor hole patterns (NMRV030) against the purchased parts, before release.
Yaw carriage sheets (5xx) are PRELIMINARY. Every dimension marked TO CONFIRM depends on the downstream bay: the floor height and width, the space under the test-section exit (the carriage plate's front edge reaches 2.3 in under it at ±15° yaw), the rail angular extents and the floor-bolt holes. The wheel-stud radii also depend on the groove depth of the purchased V-wheels. Measure the tunnel and check the wheel vendor drawing before machining.
