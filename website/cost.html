Ordering checklist

  1. Materials: balance 7075-T651; sting and spindle 42CrMo (the standard Chinese grade equal to 4140); everything else 6061-T6.
  2. Drawings: attach the PDF (Drawings page; cad/drawings/WT-6C-xxx.pdf in the project) and choose "per drawing" tolerances for the balance, sting, spindle, housing, caps, puck, sleeve, cheeks, racks, rails and drive bracket.
  3. Drawing-only features: rack teeth (gear data tables) and the yaw rail V-edges.
  4. Finish: clear anodize II; hard anodize III on the yaw rails; none on the balance; black oxide on the steel parts.
  5. Quantities: head plate, cheek, cross spacer, drag and thrust uprights, axle and 2 in pulley x2; wheel spacers x4 each (the thrust pulley is the same part as the 2 in pulley).
  6. Yaw carriage (5xx): order as a second batch once the bay floor is measured; everything else can be built and calibrated first.
  7. Build time and coupons: economy saves ~8 % if 11 days is fine; apply the coupons at checkout.

About the two staff-quoted parts

  • WT-6C-307 base tube: a 31 in length of 3 x 1.5 x 0.125 in rectangular tube. The instant quoter prices parts milled from a solid block, so a person prices it from bought tube. Easy alternative: buy the tube cut to length locally and drill the 18 holes in a school shop.
  • WT-6C-402 pitch rack: its gear teeth are cut by wire EDM and two holes go into its edge, so it is priced from the drawing. Attach WT-6C-402 and ask for EDM teeth.

How the estimates were made

Method, assumptions and sources
  • For each part the script reads the STEP file: the bounding box gives the stock size (plate rounded up to a stock thickness), and stock minus part volume gives the roughing time (2.5 in³/min aluminium, 0.5 in³/min steel).
  • Added: 1.5 min per hole, slot or thread; 0.4 h per setup; CAM programming 0.3-4 h; the special operations from the drawings (wire EDM, grinding, gun drilling, inspection).
  • Rates ($/h): China 10-20 with 20 % of the programming time, calibrated on 2026-10-03 to the real JLCCNC quote (the quote falls inside the estimate range for 16 of 22 comparable parts); EDM 20-40, grinding 30-55. SendCutSend 40-70; US marketplace 95-150; local shop 65-105.
  • Material as small cut pieces: 6061 $8-14/lb, 7075 $15-26/lb, 4140 $6-12/lb (China 45 % of that).
  • Shipping from China: DDP express at $8-15 per chargeable kg (quote 2 came to $13/kg). Duty follows JLCCNC's published pre-collection rates; tariffs change, so check at order time.
  • Files: machining_cost_estimate.py, machining_cost_estimate.csv, jlccnc_quote_2026-10-03.json. The quote package is the project folder Quote-Part-Step-Files/ (36 STEP files + parts_manifest.csv), made by tools/collect_quote_step_files.py.

Sources: JLCCNC, JLCCNC US tariff FAQ, JLCCNC reviews, PCBWay forum quote comparison, RapidDirect, SendCutSend CNC guidelines, SendCutSend CNC, Protolabs Network, Xometry partner network, Xometry CNC, China-US DDP express rates.
