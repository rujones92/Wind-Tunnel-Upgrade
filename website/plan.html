Open decisions

  • Downstream duct floor height and width at the yaw rails (assumed 11.35 in below the centreline, 36 in wide).
  • Space under the test-section exit: at ±15° yaw the carriage plate and inner rail reach about 2.3 in under the test-section floor. Either confirm the space is open or trim the carriage.
  • Side-load full scales (8 lbf / 10 in-lbf / 30 in-lbf assumed) and the largest model span to be rolled.
  • Station GUI software stack (the ADC is decided: 6x NAU7802; ADS1263 is the upgrade path).
  • Gearbox-adapter wire window (WT-6C-204) for the 270° cable twist of calibration mode; encoder multi-turn homing on the pitch and yaw pinions.
  • Small mounts not yet designed: pitch/yaw encoder mounts, SCL3300 inclinometer mount, limit-switch brackets and hard stops, Pico sensor-node mount, J0 connector bracket (they depend on the purchased switches and sensor boards).
  • Whether motion is allowed with the wind on.
