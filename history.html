Why aluminum and not a printed balance

Printed PPS-CF can reach the stiffness needed if printed on its side with thicker sections, and a hybrid with metal end fittings was fully designed. But polymer creep (several % of the reading within minutes in published data and estimates), moisture and temperature sensitivity are of the same size as the cruise drag signal, and no filament maker publishes creep data for printed parts. The aluminum balance avoids all of that; printed parts are used for models, fairings and fixtures instead.
